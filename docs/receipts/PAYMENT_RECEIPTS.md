# Governed payment receipts

OPS-1 receipts are immutable proof of money received, not proof that an order merely exists or changed status. Each receipt is anchored to one authoritative cash or bank settlement. An issued Sales Invoice is the principal document; its Sales Order context is retained. Partial settlements produce separate receipts with the received amount and remaining-balance snapshot.

References are generated by a concurrency-safe company sequence. Settlement identity makes retries idempotent. The browser never inserts receipts or invents references, and reprint/PDF actions never post payment again. Issued evidence cannot be deleted or edited; corrections retain an explicit void/reversal relationship.

POS remains `non_fiscal`. EN uses “Non-fiscal receipt” and “Proof of payment”; PT uses “Recibo não fiscal” and “Comprovativo de pagamento”. Printing supports 58 mm, 80 mm, and A4/PDF through the browser/operating-system print dialog. Silent printer discovery is not claimed.

The snapshot includes company/customer identity, business references, payment evidence, destination, and POS lines where available. Empty logos and nonexistent tax, tendered, or change fields are omitted.
